## Project Description
My project will be based on a childhood video game I used to play on my ipad. You control a little pixelated bird in a plane as he flies through different levels, fighting
different enemies and unique bosses. The environment of each level is different, with some that I remember being in space, with others being in amuesment parks and desserts.

Needs to have:
- 1 useable character (Done)
- 3 different normal enemy types (Done)
- 1 boss (Done)
- sound effects (Done)
- background music (Done)
- changing background (Done)
- main menu (Done)
- 1 playable level (Done)
- attack projectiles (enemies and friendly) (Done)
- level should be ~ 2 minutes long (Done)
 
Nice to have
- more than 1 playable character (2 - 3 would be nice)
- more than 1 level
- 1 or 2 new bosses with the the addition of each level
- 2 - 3 new normal enemy types in every new level
- different background with each new level (Partially done. Can change background, but no new level)
- implementing health packs and powerups
- tutorial
